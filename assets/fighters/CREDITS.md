# Fighter sprites

“Base for fighting or beat'em up” by **KeyZip**, commissioned and shared by **CapitanRasputin**.

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/base-for-fighting-or-beatem-up
- Author page: https://capitanrasputin.itch.io/base-for-fighting-or-beat-em-up
- Original sheet: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/list.png
- License: **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- `keyzip.png` is the unmodified source sheet. This game adds frame rectangles, foot origins, animation timing, mirroring and runtime player tinting in `keyzip.json` and `shared/sprites.js`.
- These assets are included as part of the game. The code's MIT license does not replace the sprite license.
